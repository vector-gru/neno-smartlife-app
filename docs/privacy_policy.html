1.Information We Collect

The App is designed to minimise the personal information required. You can browse the full product catalogue without providing any information at all. Information is only requested when you choose to interact with the store.

Information you provide directly:

  • Name and phone number — entered voluntarily when you save a favourite, add an item to your cart, or submit a purchase request. Your phone number acts as your unique identifier in place of a traditional account.
  • Chat messages — content of messages you send to the store through the in-app chat feature.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device push notification token — generated by Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver order status notifications to your device. This token identifies your device, not you personally.
  • App usage data — products you view, items added to your cart or favourites list, and purchase requests submitted, stored in association with your phone number.
No passwords or accounts. The App does not use traditional accounts, email addresses, or passwords. Your phone number is the only identifier used to retrieve your history and activity.

2.How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to operate and improve the App:

  • To process and manage your purchase requests and order history.
  • To enable in-app chat between you and the store team.
  • To send push notifications about the status of your orders (confirmation, shipping updates, completion).
  • To retrieve your cart, favourites, and purchase history when you return to the App.
  • To allow the store administrator to respond to your requests and provide customer service.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose beyond operating the App and serving your in-store requests.

3.Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore database and Cloud Storage), a cloud platform provided by Google LLC. Firebase applies industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and encryption at rest.

Product images uploaded by the store administrator are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage. Your personal data (name, phone number, chat messages, order records) is stored in Firebase Firestore.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to contact us immediately if you suspect any unauthorised access to your information.

4.Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services to function:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Storage, Authentication, Cloud Messaging) — for database storage, file storage, user identity management, and push notifications. Governed by the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts (Poppins) — loaded from Google's servers to display the App's typeface. Google may log the request; no personal App data is shared. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social media SDKs.

5.Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Your data is only shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • With the store administrator — your name, phone number, chat messages, and order details are visible to the Neno SmartLife store team so they can fulfil your requests and communicate with you.
  • With Firebase (Google) — as described above, solely as the infrastructure provider that stores and delivers your data.
  • When required by law — we may disclose information if required to do so by a court order or other legal obligation under applicable law.

6.Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as it is needed to provide the services described in this policy — primarily to maintain your order history and allow you to access past purchases.

If you would like your data deleted, please contact us using the details in Section 10. We will remove your name, phone number, chat history, and associated records from our database within a reasonable time, subject to any legal obligations that may require us to retain certain records.

7.Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing.
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a structured, readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly (see Section 10). We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8.Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the App, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's features or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
