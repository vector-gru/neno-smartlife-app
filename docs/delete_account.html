Delete Your Account

Neno SmartLife

We're sorry to see you go. You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time — instantly from inside the app, or by contacting us directly.

⚠️
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be erased. If you return with the same phone number it will be treated as a completely new account.
Instant

Option 1 — Delete directly from the app

The fastest way. Your data is deleted immediately, no waiting required.

  1. Open the Neno SmartLife app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Account tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Account page.
  4. Tap the "Clear My Data" button. It will ask you to confirm before anything is deleted.
  5. Confirm by tapping "Delete Everything" — your account and all personal data are removed instantly.

As it appears in the app

Clear History
Clear My Data

"Clear History" removes activity only (orders, chat, favourites) but keeps your name and phone.
"Clear My Data" removes everything, including your name, phone number, and full account.

Note: "Clear My Data" is only visible when you have a saved identity on the account (name + phone number). If you see only "Clear History", your account has no personal identity stored — your activity data will still be cleared.
🗑️
What will be deleted
  • Your account — your identity record, identified by phone number
  • Your name & phone number — all personally identifiable information
  • Saved favourites & cart — all items you saved or added to cart
  • Order & request history — all past and active purchase requests
  • Chat conversations — your full message history with the store
  • Interest signals — product interactions tracked to your phone number
  • Device push token — used to deliver order notifications to your device
Legal retention: Some anonymised transaction records may be kept for a limited period where required by applicable law (e.g. accounting purposes). No personally identifiable information is retained beyond what the law requires.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Can't access the app, or prefer we handle it? Send us a deletion request and we'll take care of it.

  1. Tap Request Account Deletion below — it opens your email app with a pre-filled message.
  2. Send the email and include your registered phone number so we can locate your account.
  3. We'll verify your identity and process the request within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation once done.
