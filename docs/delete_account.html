Delete Your Account

Neno SmartLife

We're sorry to see you go. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time — no questions asked.

⚠️
This action is permanent. Once your account and data are deleted they cannot be recovered. All your order history, favourites, and chat messages will be permanently removed.
🗑️
What will be deleted
  • Your account — your identity in our system, identified by your phone number
  • Your name — the name you entered when making requests
  • Your phone number — your primary identifier used across the app
  • Your profile & activity data — cart items, saved favourites, and preferences
  • Your order & request history — all past and active purchase requests
  • Your chat messages — the full conversation history with the store
  • Your device push token — used to deliver order notifications to your device
Note: Some records may be retained for a limited period where required by law (e.g. transaction records for accounting purposes). All personally identifiable information will be removed or anonymised.
📩
How to request deletion
  1. Tap the Request Account Deletion button below. It will open your email app with a pre-filled message.
  2. Send the email from the address associated with your account, or include your registered phone number in the message body so we can identify your account.
  3. We will verify your identity, process your request, and send a confirmation once your data has been deleted.
